Lob privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Lob is made by Briqvo (Abdullah Ventures LLC). Contact: briqvo@gmail.com.

The short version

Your events stay on your phone. Nothing is uploaded to us, and there is no account.

What Lob keeps

The events you create: the event name, the club name if you add one, the players' names as you typed them, and the scores. They are stored on your phone only. Delete one event or all of them in the app; uninstalling Lob deletes everything.

Sharing

  • The result card is an image Lob makes on your phone and hands to the app you choose.
  • The scoreboard link carries the event (names, rounds and scores) inside the link itself, in the part after "#". Browsers do not send that part to any server, and the page that opens has no cookies or trackers. Anyone who has the link can see the names and scores in it, so send it to the group it is for. Like any website, the company that hosts the page file may log the address of the device that asks for the file; it cannot see the event.

Other people's names

You type other players' names into Lob. Use first names or nicknames if someone would rather not be named in a link.

Purchases

The organiser plan is sold through Google Play (or the App Store) and checked through RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase receipt. See https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

What Lob does not do

No ads, no analytics, no tracking across apps, no selling of data, no account.

Children

Lob is not directed at children under 13.

Your rights

Briqvo (Abdullah Ventures LLC) is responsible for the data described in this policy (the "controller" under EU and UK data-protection law). We use it only to provide the app you chose to use. Wherever you live, you can ask us what data we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it, by writing to briqvo@gmail.com; because almost everything stays on your phone, we usually hold nothing that identifies you. If you live in the European Economic Area or the UK, you can also complain to your local data-protection authority. Our service providers (Google (Play and Play Billing), RevenueCat) may process data in the United States and other countries, under their own data-protection terms.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
