Sept privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Sept is made by Briqvo (Abdullah Ventures LLC). Contact: briqvo@gmail.com.

The short version

Your practice stays on your phone. The one exception is when you ask for an answer to be marked: that answer is sent to be marked and is not kept by us. There is no account.

What stays on your phone

Your answers to listening and reading questions, mock results, level estimates, written drafts, and markings you received. Recordings of your spoken answers are kept on your phone and replaced when you record again; a copy is sent only when you ask for that answer to be scored (below). You can delete everything in Settings, Reset my progress; uninstalling Sept deletes it too.

When you ask for an answer to be marked

  • A written answer is sent, with the task it answers, to Sept's marking service, which passes it to Anthropic (the maker of the Claude AI model) to be marked.
  • A spoken answer is sent as an audio recording to Sept's marking service, turned into text by Cloudflare Workers AI, and the text is passed to Anthropic to be marked.
  • Sept's service stores neither the answer, the recording nor the marking. It keeps only a random install ID and a count of markings per day, to apply the free and fair-use limits.
  • Anthropic and Cloudflare process the data to provide the service under their own terms and may keep it for a limited time: https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy and https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/.
  • Do not put personal details you would not want processed (full name, address, ID numbers) into a practice answer.

Microphone

Sept uses the microphone only while you record a speaking answer, and only after you tap record.

Purchases

Plans are sold through Google Play (or the App Store) and checked through RevenueCat, which receives the random install ID and your purchase receipt. See https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

What Sept does not do

No ads, no analytics, no tracking across apps, no selling of data, no account.

Not an official test

Sept is an independent practice app. It is not affiliated with the organisations that run TEF Canada or TCF Canada, or with the Government of Canada. Levels shown are estimates for practice.

Children

Sept is not directed at children under 13.

Your rights

Briqvo (Abdullah Ventures LLC) is responsible for the data described in this policy (the "controller" under EU and UK data-protection law). We use it only to provide the app you chose to use, and to keep its free limits fair and stop abuse. Wherever you live, you can ask us what data we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it, by writing to briqvo@gmail.com; because almost everything stays on your phone, we usually hold nothing that identifies you. If you live in the European Economic Area or the UK, you can also complain to your local data-protection authority. Our service providers (Google (Play and Play Billing), RevenueCat, Cloudflare and Anthropic) may process data in the United States and other countries, under their own data-protection terms.
