Duet privacy policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Duet is made by Briqvo (Abdullah Ventures LLC). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the Duet app for Android and iPhone.

The short version

  • Conversations and saved phrases stay on your phone. We don't keep a copy.
  • When you speak in a conversation, that recording is sent to our server, turned into text, translated and spoken back. Recordings are never stored: each exists only in memory while it's translated, a few seconds.
  • Photos of menus and signs are never stored either: one shrunk photo is sent, read, translated and forgotten. The photo on your phone is deleted after translating.
  • Typed text is translated on your phone on Android, without the internet, once the language is downloaded. Otherwise it's sent to our server to translate and isn't kept.
  • There's no sign-up. Duet never asks for your name, email or phone number. No ads, no analytics.
  • Plans are bought through Google Play or the App Store. We use RevenueCat to handle purchases.

Conversations

When one of you taps the microphone and speaks, Duet records that turn (up to 30 seconds) and sends it to our server with the two languages and the last few lines of the conversation as text (so pronouns and tone come out right).

  • Our server (on Cloudflare) holds the recording only in memory while it's processed. It's never written to storage, logs or backups. The server counts how many seconds were spoken, for your voice minutes.
  • Cloudflare Workers AI runs the AI for everyone, including free trials, unless a provider below is used for a paying subscriber. These are open AI models hosted by Cloudflare on its own network. It receives the recording (to turn it into words), the text to translate, photos you translate, and the translation (for the voice you hear, in English and Spanish) and sends back the result. Cloudflare states that it doesn't use this content to train AI models. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.
  • For paying subscribers, we may instead use: OpenAI (speech-to-text), which receives the recording and returns the words said. See OpenAI's privacy policy. OpenAI states that data sent through its API is not used to train its models by default.
  • For paying subscribers, we may instead use: Anthropic (Claude), which receives the text (never the recording) and translates it. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, data sent through its API is not used to train its models. See Anthropic's privacy policy.
  • For paying subscribers, we may instead use: Google Cloud Text-to-Speech, which receives the translated text and returns the voice you hear. See Google's privacy policy.

These providers may keep requests for a limited time for safety, abuse prevention and legal reasons, under their own terms. The person you're talking to is speaking into your phone too, so please let them know you're using a translator (Duet's first screen on their side says so in their language).

The finished conversation (both sides' words and translations) is saved in the app on your phone so you can read it again or make a share card. You can delete one conversation or all of them at any time. The share card is an image made on your phone; it's only shared if you choose to share it.

Photos of menus and signs

Duet asks for the camera only when you open camera translate, after explaining why. When you take a photo, a smaller copy is sent to our server and on to our AI provider, which reads the text and translates it. Neither is stored by us; the photo and its copy are deleted from your phone after translating. Please don't photograph documents with personal details.

Typed text and offline languages

On Android, Duet uses Google's ML Kit to translate typed text on your phone. It downloads a language pack (about 30 MB) from Google the first time you pick a language, on Wi-Fi; after that, typed text never leaves your phone. Until a pack is downloaded, and on iPhone for now, typed text is sent to our server and translated by our AI provider, and isn't kept. Saved phrases and the phrasebook's translations are stored on your phone.

When the phone reads a translation aloud with its own voice (for typed text, phrases and photos), that's your phone's text-to-speech, which works on the device.

Microphone

Duet asks for the microphone the first time you start a conversation, after explaining why. It listens only while a turn is being recorded (the button shows it, and a turn stops by itself after 30 seconds), never in the background. You can turn the microphone off at any time in your phone's settings; typing, phrases and photos still work.

Your Duet account

Duet has no sign-up. The first time you use it, our server creates a random account ID and keeps, for that ID: how many voice seconds, photos and cloud texts you've used this day, week and month, and whether a plan is active. The ID is stored on your phone.

Our server's provider (Cloudflare) handles network information such as IP addresses to deliver requests and stop attacks, and our server keeps short-lived error logs that never contain your recordings, photos or words.

Plans and purchases

Purchases go through Google Play or the App Store; we never see your card details. RevenueCat, our purchase provider, receives your Duet account ID and your purchase history so the app and our server know your plan. See RevenueCat's privacy policy. If you start a free trial, Duet sets a local reminder on your phone for two days before it ends (no push service involved). The Travel Week is a one-time purchase that ends after 7 days; nothing renews.

Children

Duet isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • Delete conversations in the app (one, or all in Settings). Uninstalling Duet deletes everything on the phone.
  • Remove downloaded language packs in Settings.
  • Our server's record for your random account ID holds only usage counts and plan status, nothing that identifies you. Uninstalling Duet removes the ID from your phone, and the record can no longer be linked to anyone. For any other request, email us from Settings → Contact us.
