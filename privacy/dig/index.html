Dig privacy policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Dig is made by Briqvo (Abdullah Ventures LLC). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the Dig app for Android and iPhone.

The short version

  • No account, no personal data. Dig never asks for your name, email, phone number or address, and has no ads.
  • When you scan an item, that photo is sent to our server and from there to Anthropic's Claude AI model to identify it and estimate its value. Our server never stores the photo.
  • Your collection (photos you saved, estimates, what you paid) stays on your phone.
  • Plans are bought through Google Play or the App Store. We use RevenueCat to handle subscriptions.

Photos of items

When you take or pick a photo, Dig makes a smaller copy (at most 1280 pixels across) and sends it to our server with the currency you value things in (for example USD). For a closer look, it sends the first photo again with the second one. Nothing else goes with them.

  • Our server (on Cloudflare) holds the photos only in memory while they are read, a few seconds. They are never written to storage, logs or backups.
  • Anthropic, our AI provider, receives the photos and our instructions, and sends back what the item is, its estimated value and selling tips. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, data sent through its API is not used to train its models; Anthropic may keep requests for a limited time for safety and legal reasons. See Anthropic's privacy policy.

Please photograph only the item: not people, documents or anything personal.

On your phone only

Your collection (each saved photo, its estimate and details, and what you paid if you typed it) and your settings are kept in the app's private storage on your phone. A scan you don't save is deleted from your phone when you leave its result. You can delete any find (open it, then "Delete from collection"). Uninstalling Dig deletes everything. We have no copy, so we can't recover it for you.

When you use "Prepare a listing", Dig copies text to your clipboard and opens the share sheet or the marketplace's website only when you tap those buttons. Dig never posts anything for you and never sees your marketplace accounts.

When you share a valuation card, the card is made on your phone and goes only where you send it.

Your Dig account

Dig has no sign-up. The first time you scan, our server creates a random account ID and keeps, for that ID: which scans were free, which scans had a closer look, how many scans you've made today and in the last hour (for the daily limits) and whether your plan is active. The ID is stored on your phone.

Our server's provider (Cloudflare) handles network information such as IP addresses to deliver requests and stop attacks, and our server keeps short-lived error logs that never contain your photos.

Plans and purchases

Plans are bought through Google Play or the App Store; we never see your payment details. RevenueCat, our subscription service, receives your Dig account ID and your purchase history for Dig, and our server asks RevenueCat whether your plan is active. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

If you start a free trial, Dig sets a reminder on your phone for 2 days before you're charged. It's a local notification: no push service is involved.

Estimates, not appraisals

Dig's values are estimates from photos. They aren't professional appraisals, authentication or grading, and we can't guarantee what an item will sell for. For valuable pieces, ask a specialist.

Children

Dig isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone: there is no account, no profile and no tracking.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your information or share it for advertising. Dig has no ads.
  • We don't keep your photos on our server, or use them for anything except valuing that item.
  • Dig has no analytics of its own at this time. If that changes, this policy will say so before it ships.

Your choices

  • Scan and save only what you choose; nothing is sent in the background.
  • Delete any saved find in the app at any time.
  • Ask us to delete your account ID's usage counters: email briqvo@gmail.com.
