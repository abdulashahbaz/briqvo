Panora privacy policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Panora is made by Briqvo (Abdullah Ventures LLC). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the Panora app for Android.

The short version

  • Your photos stay on your phone. You choose them with Android's photo picker; Panora edits them and saves your slides on the device. It has no account and never uploads them.
  • Panora shows a banner ad in the template gallery, and a video ad when you choose to watch one to unlock a premium template. Ads come from Google AdMob, may use your device's advertising ID and similar data, and where the law requires it we ask for your consent first. Panora Pro removes ads.
  • Purchases go through Google Play. We use RevenueCat to check whether you own Panora Pro.
  • Panora downloads new templates from our server. That request carries no information about you or your photos.

Your photos

Panora only sees the photos you pick in Android's photo picker, so it needs no permission to read your gallery. It uses them to:

  • draw your carousel, photo dump, before & after or story on the screen;
  • work out the five main colours of your photos for the colour palette, on the device;
  • save the finished slides as new JPEG images in a "Panora" album on your phone. On Android 8 and 9 only, saving asks for storage permission; newer Android versions need no permission for this.

Your designs are not stored after you leave the editor, and nothing about your photos (not the photos, their names, locations or dates) is sent to us or anyone else. Sharing happens only when you choose a destination in your phone's share sheet.

Templates

The app checks a static file on our server for new templates at most every six hours, or when you pull to refresh the gallery. It is an ordinary download: we receive only what any web server receives (your IP address, at the time of the request, in standard server logs), and nothing about you or your photos. The templates are kept on your phone so the app also works offline.

Ads

The free version shows one banner ad in the template gallery, starting after your first export, and never in the editor. If you choose to unlock a premium template, Panora shows a short video ad (a "rewarded ad"). Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, general device information, an approximate location derived from your IP address, and how you interact with ads. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, and wherever else the law requires, Panora shows Google's consent form before any ad is requested (the first time an ad could appear, not when you first open the app), and you can change your choice any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

How Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Purchases

Panora Pro is a one-time purchase through Google Play; we never see your payment details. RevenueCat, our purchase service, receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history for Panora so the app knows you own Pro, including after you reinstall. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your personal information.
  • We don't upload, look at, or keep copies of your photos or designs.
  • Panora has no analytics of its own at this time. If that changes, this policy will say so before it ships.

Children

Panora is not directed at children under 13, and ad requests are limited to content suitable for general audiences (rated PG).

Your choices and rights

You can change ad consent in Settings, reset your advertising ID, or buy Panora Pro to stop ads entirely. Uninstalling Panora removes everything it stored on your phone except the slides you saved to your gallery, which are yours. For any privacy question or request, including deleting purchase records held by RevenueCat, contact us at briqvo@gmail.com.
