Ibra privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Ibra is made by Briqvo (Abdullah Ventures LLC). Contact: briqvo@gmail.com.

Ibra stores what you enter (your shot schedule, doses, injection sites, side effects, weight, protein and water, and any medicine name you type) on your phone only. Nothing is uploaded, there is no account, and Briqvo cannot see your data. Delete it in Settings or by uninstalling Ibra.

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your phone. The doctor's report is a PDF made on your phone; it goes only where you choose to share it.

Ibra Plus is sold through Google Play (or the App Store) and checked through RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase receipt, never your health data (https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy).

No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no selling of data. Ibra is not a medical device and gives no medical advice. Not directed at children under 13.

Your rights

Briqvo (Abdullah Ventures LLC) is responsible for the data described in this policy (the "controller" under EU and UK data-protection law). We use it only to provide the app you chose to use. Wherever you live, you can ask us what data we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it, by writing to briqvo@gmail.com; because almost everything stays on your phone, we usually hold nothing that identifies you. If you live in the European Economic Area or the UK, you can also complain to your local data-protection authority. Our service providers (Google (Play and Play Billing), RevenueCat) may process data in the United States and other countries, under their own data-protection terms.
