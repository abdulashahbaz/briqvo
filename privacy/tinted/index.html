Tinted privacy policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Tinted is made by Briqvo (Abdullah Ventures LLC). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the Tinted app for Android.

The short version

  • Tinted has no account. Wallpapers, icons and widgets are drawn on your phone, and your list of apps never leaves it.
  • Tinted downloads its theme catalog from our server. That download contains no information about you.
  • Tinted shows ads (from Google AdMob) unless you buy "Tinted Pro". Ads may use your device's advertising ID and similar data, and where the law requires it we ask for your consent first.
  • Purchases go through Google Play. We use RevenueCat to check whether you own Tinted Pro.

What stays on your phone

  • Your apps. To theme your app icons, Tinted reads the list of apps that have a home-screen icon (their names, package names and categories) and draws a new icon for the ones you pick. This happens only on your phone; the list is never sent to us or anyone else.
  • Wallpaper. Tinted draws the theme's wallpaper on your phone and sets it through Android's wallpaper service. It never reads or uploads your current wallpaper.
  • Widgets. The Clock and Date widgets use your phone's own clock and calendar settings. They don't use your location or any account.
  • Your settings. The theme you applied, the symbols you chose for your icons, the themes you unlocked and the last theme catalog Tinted downloaded are stored in Tinted's private storage on your phone. Uninstalling Tinted deletes them.

"Share my setup" images are drawn from the theme (its wallpaper, name and code). They are only shared when you choose a destination in your phone's share sheet, and they contain nothing from your phone.

The theme catalog

To show new themes without an app update, Tinted downloads a list of themes from our content delivery network (Cloudflare) when it starts, when you pull to refresh the gallery, and when you type a theme code it doesn't know yet. Like any web request, this reveals your IP address to the server; Tinted sends no identifiers, account or usage data with it, and we don't use the server's request logs to identify anyone. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Ads

The free version shows a banner on the theme gallery, starting after you apply your first theme, and never while you apply one. Pro themes can be unlocked for 24 hours by watching a rewarded ad, which only plays when you tap to watch it. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, general device information, an approximate location derived from your IP address, and how you interact with ads. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, and wherever else the law requires, Tinted shows Google's consent form before any ad is requested, and you can change your choice any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

How Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Purchases

"Tinted Pro" is a one-time purchase through Google Play; we never see your payment details. RevenueCat, our purchase service, receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history for Tinted so the app knows that you own Pro, including after you reinstall. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Ratings

Tinted may ask, at most once, whether you'd like to rate it, using Google Play's own review window. Your rating and review go to Google Play under its terms; Tinted doesn't see them.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your personal information.
  • We don't upload your list of apps, your wallpaper or anything else from your phone.
  • Tinted has no analytics of its own at this time. If that changes, this policy will say so before it ships.

Children

Tinted is not directed at children under 13, and ad requests are limited to content suitable for general audiences (rated PG).

Your choices and rights

You can change ad consent in Settings, reset your advertising ID, or buy Tinted Pro to stop ads entirely. Removing Tinted's icons and widgets from your home screen, or uninstalling Tinted, removes everything it stored. For any privacy question or request, including deleting purchase records held by RevenueCat, contact us at briqvo@gmail.com.
