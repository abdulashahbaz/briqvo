DoFirst privacy policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

DoFirst is made by Briqvo (Abdullah Ventures LLC). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the DoFirst app for Android.

The short version

  • Your tasks, reminders, locked apps, schedules and stats stay on your phone. DoFirst has no account and no server of its own.
  • To lock apps, DoFirst's Accessibility service notices only which app comes to the front of your screen. It can't read what's on your screen, and nothing it sees is stored or sent anywhere.
  • There are no ads and no analytics.
  • If you buy DoFirst Pro, the purchase goes through Google Play, and we use RevenueCat to check that you own it.

Your tasks and settings

Everything you put into DoFirst (tasks, notes, subtasks, due dates, repeat rules, the apps you choose to lock, lock schedules and Pro options) is stored in DoFirst's private storage on your phone. So is the history of tasks you've finished, which powers the stats screen and the weekly recap. Uninstalling DoFirst deletes all of it. None of it is sent to us or to anyone else.

Focus lock and the Accessibility service

Focus lock uses Android's AccessibilityService API. You turn it on yourself in Android's Accessibility settings, after DoFirst shows you a screen explaining what it does and you tap "Agree".

  • What it accesses: the name (package name) of the app that comes to the front of your screen, whenever focus lock is on, including when DoFirst is closed.
  • Why: to tell whether that app is one you chose to lock while a task is locking it, and if so, to cover it with DoFirst's block screen.
  • What it never accesses: the contents of your screen, what you type, your messages, your passwords or anything inside other apps. The service is configured so it cannot read window content.
  • Storage and sharing: none. The app name is checked on your phone at that moment and then discarded. It is not stored, logged, sent to us or shared with anyone.
  • Turning it off: any time, in Settings → Accessibility → DoFirst focus lock. Everything else in DoFirst keeps working.

To show you the apps you can lock, DoFirst lists the apps on your phone that have a launcher icon. This list is read on your phone only.

Photo proof (Pro)

If you turn on photo proof, finishing an urgent task asks you to take a photo first. The photo is taken by your phone's camera app and saved in DoFirst's private storage, where only DoFirst can see it, and shown as a thumbnail in your stats. DoFirst doesn't ask for camera permission, and the photo is never uploaded. Undoing that task's completion deletes its photo, and uninstalling DoFirst deletes all of them.

Notifications and alarms

Reminders are scheduled with Android's alarm service on your phone and shown as local notifications, as is the optional weekly recap (tasks done and time your apps stayed locked). DoFirst asks for notification permission, and for "Alarms & reminders" so reminders ring on time, only after explaining why. Nothing about your reminders is sent anywhere.

Share cards

When you tap Share on a win, DoFirst draws an image on your phone (for example "2 apps were locked for 3h 12m until I finished: Thesis chapter 2", with the names of the apps you locked) and opens your phone's share sheet. It is only shared where you choose to send it.

Purchases

DoFirst Pro is a one-time purchase through Google Play; we never see your payment details. RevenueCat, our purchase service, receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history for DoFirst so the app knows Pro is yours, including after you reinstall. See RevenueCat's privacy policy. DoFirst only contacts RevenueCat to check purchases; it never sends your tasks, apps or stats.

Reviews

DoFirst may show Google Play's in-app review prompt once, after you finish a task. Google Play handles it; DoFirst doesn't see whether or how you rated it.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or share your personal information.
  • We don't collect your tasks, the apps you use, or anything the Accessibility service sees.
  • DoFirst has no ads and no analytics at this time. If that changes, this policy will say so before it ships.

Children

DoFirst is not directed at children under 13.

Your choices and rights

You can turn focus lock off in Accessibility settings, turn notifications off, delete any task, or uninstall DoFirst to delete everything it stored. For any privacy question or request, including deleting purchase records held by RevenueCat, contact us at briqvo@gmail.com.
