Aha privacy policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Aha is made by Briqvo (Abdullah Ventures LLC). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the Aha app for Android and iPhone. Aha is for students aged 13 and up.

The short version

  • No account, no personal data. Aha never asks for your name, email, phone number, school or age, and has no ads.
  • When you solve a problem, that one cropped photo is sent to our server and from there to Anthropic's Claude AI model to read and explain it. The photo is never stored: not on our server, and not on your phone after it has been sent.
  • Your solved problems (the text of each problem and its steps) stay on your phone.
  • If you share a solution, its text (never the photo) is kept on our server for 90 days so the link works.
  • Plans are bought through Google Play or the App Store. We use RevenueCat to handle subscriptions.

Photos of problems

When you take or pick a photo, you crop it to one problem. Aha sends only the cropped part (at most 1568 pixels across) to our server, with your short note if you type one and your phone's language. Nothing else goes with it.

  • On your phone, the photo and the crop are deleted as soon as they have been sent.
  • Our server (on Cloudflare) holds the photo only in memory while it is read, a few seconds. It is never written to storage, logs or backups.
  • Anthropic, our AI provider, receives the photo and our instructions, and sends back the problem's text, the steps and the answer. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, data sent through its API is not used to train its models; Anthropic may keep requests for a limited time for safety and legal reasons. See Anthropic's privacy policy.

Please photograph only the problem: not people, names or anything personal.

Questions to the tutor and practice problems

When you ask about a step or ask for a practice problem, Aha sends the solution's text and your question to our server and to Anthropic in the same way. Conversations aren't stored: they disappear when you leave the screen. Please don't type personal information into questions; the tutor is told never to ask for any.

On your phone only

Your history (each problem's text, its steps and answer, how many steps you opened and which you worked out yourself) and your settings are kept in the app's private storage on your phone. You can delete one problem (long press it in History) or all of them (Settings, Clear history). Uninstalling Aha deletes everything. We have no copy, so we can't recover it for you.

Shared links

If you tap Share, the solution's text (the problem, the steps and the answer, never the photo or anything about you) is saved on our server under a random link and shown on a public web page for 90 days, then deleted automatically. Anyone with the link can see it, and search engines are asked not to index it. To have a link removed sooner, or to report one, email briqvo@gmail.com with the link.

Your Aha account

Aha has no sign-up. The first time you solve, our server creates a random account ID and keeps, for that ID: how many problems and questions you've used today and in the last hour (for the daily limits) and whether your plan is active. The ID is stored on your phone.

Our server's provider (Cloudflare) handles network information such as IP addresses to deliver requests and stop attacks, and our server keeps short-lived error logs that never contain your photos.

Plans and purchases

Plans are bought through Google Play or the App Store; we never see your payment details. RevenueCat, our subscription service, receives your Aha account ID and your purchase history for Aha, and our server asks RevenueCat whether your plan is active. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

If you start a free trial, Aha sets a reminder on your phone for 2 days before you're charged. It's a local notification: no push service is involved.

Children

Aha is for students aged 13 and up and is rated for that age. It doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, of any age: there is no account, no profile and no tracking. If you're a parent and believe your child under 13 has shared a link with personal information in it, email us and we'll delete it.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your information or share it for advertising. Aha has no ads, on the free plan or any other.
  • We don't keep your photos, or use them for anything except solving that problem.
  • Aha has no analytics of its own at this time. If that changes, this policy will say so before it ships.

Your choices

  • Solve, ask and share only what you choose; nothing is sent in the background.
  • Delete your history in the app at any time.
  • Ask us to delete your account ID's usage counters, or a shared link: email briqvo@gmail.com.
