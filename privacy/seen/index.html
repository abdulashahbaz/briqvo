Seen privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Seen is made by Briqvo (Abdullah Ventures LLC). Contact: briqvo@gmail.com.

The short version

Seen reads the chat you give it on your phone, counts it, and deletes its copy. Nothing you import is sent to us or anyone else. There is no account.

What Seen reads

When you share or open an exported chat, Seen reads the file on your device to count messages, words, emoji, times and names. This happens entirely on your phone. The copy of the file Seen made is deleted as soon as your recap is ready, or right away if the file can't be read.

What Seen keeps

A saved recap stores, on your phone only: the chat name you chose, participants' display names as they appear in the export, and the counts shown on the slides (for example message totals, top emoji, the most used words). It never stores the messages themselves. You can delete one recap or all of them in Settings; uninstalling Seen deletes everything.

Purchases

The one-time unlock is sold through Google Play (or the App Store) and checked through RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase receipt; it does not receive your chats. See https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

Sharing

When you share a slide, Seen makes an image and hands it to the app you choose. What happens next is up to you and that app.

What Seen does not do

No ads, no analytics of your chats, no tracking across apps, no selling of data, no account.

Children

Seen is not directed at children under 13.

Your rights

Briqvo (Abdullah Ventures LLC) is responsible for the data described in this policy (the "controller" under EU and UK data-protection law). We use it only to provide the app you chose to use. Wherever you live, you can ask us what data we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it, by writing to briqvo@gmail.com; because almost everything stays on your phone, we usually hold nothing that identifies you. If you live in the European Economic Area or the UK, you can also complain to your local data-protection authority. Our service providers (Google (Play and Play Billing), RevenueCat) may process data in the United States and other countries, under their own data-protection terms.
