Sejour privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Sejour is made by Briqvo (Abdullah Ventures LLC). Contact: briqvo@gmail.com.

Sejour keeps your answers, practice exam results and language choice on your phone only, to show your progress. Nothing is uploaded and there is no account. Reset your progress in Settings or uninstall the app to delete it.

Purchases are made through Google Play (or the App Store) and checked through RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase receipt (https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy). A monthly plan's reminder before renewal is a local notification on your phone. When you share a score card, Sejour makes an image and hands it to the app you choose.

No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no selling of data. Not directed at children under 13.

Your rights

Briqvo (Abdullah Ventures LLC) is responsible for the data described in this policy (the "controller" under EU and UK data-protection law). We use it only to provide the app you chose to use. Wherever you live, you can ask us what data we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it, by writing to briqvo@gmail.com; because almost everything stays on your phone, we usually hold nothing that identifies you. If you live in the European Economic Area or the UK, you can also complain to your local data-protection authority. Our service providers (Google (Play and Play Billing), RevenueCat) may process data in the United States and other countries, under their own data-protection terms.
