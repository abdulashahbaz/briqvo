PassPic privacy policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

PassPic is made by Briqvo (Abdullah Ventures LLC). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the PassPic app for Android.

The short version

  • Your photos stay on your phone. PassPic measures, crops and finishes them on the device. It has no account and never uploads a photo.
  • PassPic has no ads.
  • Purchases go through Google Play. We use RevenueCat to check whether you own "PassPic Unlimited".

Camera and photos

PassPic asks for the camera only when you open the camera screen, to take your passport, visa or ID photo and coach you with live hints (face position, light, head angle, eyes open). Face detection and the person outline used to measure your head and replace the background run on your phone with Google's ML Kit models bundled in the app; they work offline and send nothing to Google or to us.

You can also pick a photo from your gallery with Android's photo picker; PassPic only receives the photo you pick.

Finished photos, print sheets and PDFs are saved where you can find them (Pictures › PassPic, or Downloads for PDFs) and are only shared when you choose a destination in your phone's share sheet.

Saved people

If you save a photo for a family member, PassPic keeps a copy of that photo and the name you typed in its private storage on your phone, so you can make photos for other documents later. Delete a person or a photo in the app at any time; uninstalling PassPic deletes all of it. None of it is sent to us or anyone else.

Purchases

"PassPic Unlimited" is a one-time purchase through Google Play; we never see your payment details. RevenueCat, our purchase service, receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history for PassPic so the app knows the purchase is owned, including after you reinstall. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your personal information.
  • We don't upload, look at or keep copies of your photos, and we don't build face templates or biometric profiles: face measurements exist only on your phone while a photo is being finished.
  • PassPic has no analytics of its own at this time. If that changes, this policy will say so before it ships.

Children

PassPic can be used by parents to make photos of their children (including baby mode). Those photos stay on the parent's phone like any other. PassPic is not directed at children and has no ads.

Your choices and rights

You can change or remove PassPic's camera access in your phone's settings at any time, and delete saved people and photos in the app. For any privacy question or request, including deleting purchase records held by RevenueCat, contact us at briqvo@gmail.com.
