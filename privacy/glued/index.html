Glued privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Glued is made by Briqvo (Abdullah Ventures LLC). Contact: briqvo@gmail.com.

The short version

Glued reads your phone's own screen-time totals on your phone and shows them back to you. Nothing is uploaded. There is no account.

What Glued reads, and why

With your permission (Android Usage access), Glued reads the usage statistics Android keeps: how long each app with a home-screen icon was open, when it was last used, how often apps were opened and how often the phone was unlocked, plus each app's name, category and icon. It uses them only to make your recap on your phone. It cannot read anything inside other apps (no messages, photos, browsing or content).

What Glued keeps

The latest recap (totals, app names and small app icons) is saved on your phone so you can reopen it. Delete it in Settings, or uninstall Glued. You can turn Usage access off at any time in Android Settings.

Purchases

The one-time unlock is sold through Google Play and checked through RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase receipt, never your usage data. See https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

Sharing

When you share a card, Glued makes an image and hands it to the app you choose.

What Glued does not do

No ads, no analytics, no tracking across apps, no selling of data, no account. Not directed at children under 13.

Your rights

Briqvo (Abdullah Ventures LLC) is responsible for the data described in this policy (the "controller" under EU and UK data-protection law). We use it only to provide the app you chose to use. Wherever you live, you can ask us what data we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it, by writing to briqvo@gmail.com; because almost everything stays on your phone, we usually hold nothing that identifies you. If you live in the European Economic Area or the UK, you can also complain to your local data-protection authority. Our service providers (Google (Play and Play Billing), RevenueCat) may process data in the United States and other countries, under their own data-protection terms.
