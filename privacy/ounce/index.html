Ounce privacy policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Ounce is made by Briqvo (Abdullah Ventures LLC). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the Ounce app for Android and iPhone.

The short version

  • Your food log, weigh-ins, goal and body details (age, height, weight, sex) stay on your phone. We never receive them.
  • When you scan a meal, that one photo is sent to our server and from there to Anthropic's Claude AI model to estimate the food. Our server never stores it. A smaller copy stays on your phone with the meal.
  • There's no sign-up. Ounce doesn't ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Plans are bought through Google Play or the App Store. We use RevenueCat to handle subscriptions.

On your phone only

Everything you log (meals, portions, calories and macros, weigh-ins), your goal and the details used to work out your daily target are kept in the app's private storage on your phone. So are the photos of meals you've logged and the portions Ounce remembers for you. Deleting a meal deletes its photo. Uninstalling Ounce deletes all of it. We have no copy, so we can't recover it for you.

Meal scans

When you take or pick a photo to scan, Ounce resizes it (at most 1024 pixels) and sends it to our server, along with a short note only if you type one. Nothing else from your log goes with it.

  • Our server (on Cloudflare) holds the photo only in memory while it is read, a few seconds. It is never written to storage, logs or backups.
  • Anthropic, our AI provider, receives the photo and our instructions, and sends back the foods, portions and estimated calories and macros. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, data sent through its API is not used to train its models; Anthropic may keep requests for a limited time for safety and legal reasons. See Anthropic's privacy policy.

Please don't scan photos of people.

Your Ounce account

Ounce has no sign-up. The first time you scan, our server creates a random account ID and keeps, for that ID: whether you've used the free scan, how many scans you've made today and in the last hour (for the daily limit), and whether your plan is active. The ID is stored on your phone.

Our server's provider (Cloudflare) handles network information such as IP addresses to deliver requests and stop attacks, and our server keeps short-lived error logs that never contain your photos.

Plans and purchases

Plans are bought through Google Play or the App Store; we never see your payment details. RevenueCat, our subscription service, receives your Ounce account ID and your purchase history for Ounce, and our server asks RevenueCat whether your plan is active. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

If you start a free trial, Ounce sets a reminder on your phone for 2 days before you're charged. It's a local notification: no push service is involved.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your information or share it for advertising. Ounce has no ads.
  • We don't receive your food log, weight or body details.
  • We don't keep your meal photos on our server, or use them for anything except reading that meal.
  • Ounce has no analytics of its own at this time. If that changes, this policy will say so before it ships.

Health

Ounce gives estimates to help you track food. It is not a medical device and doesn't give medical advice. Ounce is for adults aged 18 and over.

Your choices and rights

You can delete any meal or weigh-in in the app at any time, or everything by uninstalling. To delete the account record our server keeps (free scan used, today's count) or purchase records held by RevenueCat, contact us at briqvo@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date before the change takes effect.
