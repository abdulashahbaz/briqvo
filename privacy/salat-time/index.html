Salat Time privacy policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Salat Time is a prayer-times app made by Abdullah Ventures LLC under the Briqvo name. This policy covers the Salat Time app for Android.

The short version

Salat Time collects no personal data. Nothing you do in the app is sent to us or to anyone else. There are no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no account.

Location

Prayer times depend on where you are, so Salat Time asks for your approximate location (never your precise location, and never in the background). It's used:

  • once, to find your city when you tap "Use my location". After that, when you open the app, Salat Time checks the phone's last known position to notice if you've moved more than 30 km;
  • while the Qibla screen is open, so Android can correct the compass for your area.

Your location is processed only on your phone. The city is found in a list built into the app, without any internet lookup. Salat Time saves the place you chose (its name and coordinates) on your phone so it can calculate times. That never leaves the device.

You can refuse location access and search for your city instead. Everything still works.

Notifications and alarms

Prayer alerts are scheduled on your phone with Android's alarm service. Salat Time asks for notification permission and, optionally, "Alarms & reminders" so alerts arrive on the exact minute. Nothing about your alerts is sent anywhere.

Settings and your records

Your settings (calculation method, Asr school, minute adjustments, alert choices, Hijri date adjustment, Ramadan mode and Sehri alarm) are stored on your phone only. So are your fasting log, your count of missed (qaza) prayers and your tasbih counts. None of it is sent anywhere, and uninstalling Salat Time deletes it.

Home-screen widgets

If you add a Salat Time widget, the app stores the next 30 days of prayer times on your phone so the widget can count down without opening the app. They stay on the phone.

Ratings and other Briqvo apps

After a finished tasbih round or a marked fast, Salat Time may ask Google Play to show its rating card, at most once. Google Play shows the card and handles anything you choose there; Salat Time isn't told whether or how you rated. The "More from Briqvo" list in Settings simply opens Google Play pages for our other apps.

Sharing

If you share a monthly timetable as an image or PDF, Salat Time creates the file on your phone and hands it to the app you pick (for example WhatsApp). What happens next is up to that app and its privacy policy. Salat Time doesn't keep a copy beyond the phone's temporary cache.

Internet

Salat Time doesn't need the internet to work and doesn't send data to any server.

Children

Salat Time doesn't collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date. If Salat Time ever starts collecting data, the app will tell you and ask first.
