Stamp privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Stamp is made by Briqvo (Abdullah Ventures LLC). Contact: briqvo@gmail.com.

The short version

To explain a letter, Stamp sends your photo of it to be read. We do not keep the photo. What stays is the explanation, on your phone only. There is no account.

What happens to a photo

  • You take or choose a photo of a letter. Stamp makes a smaller copy, sends it over an encrypted connection to Stamp's reading service, and deletes its own copies from the phone straight away. A photo you chose from your gallery is not changed or deleted.
  • The reading service passes the photo to Anthropic (the maker of the Claude AI model), which reads it and writes the explanation. The service then sends the explanation back to your phone.
  • Stamp's service stores neither the photo nor the explanation, and does not log them. It keeps only a random install ID and a count of letters read, to apply the free and fair-use limits.
  • Anthropic processes the photo to provide the service under its own terms and may keep it for a limited time: https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy. Official letters often contain names, addresses and reference numbers; if you do not want part of a letter processed, cover it before you take the photo.

What stays on your phone

For each letter: who sent it, a short title, the explanation, the date and amount found, the steps, and the reply draft. Never the photo. Delete one letter or all of them in the app; uninstalling Stamp deletes everything.

Camera, photos and notifications

The camera is used only when you tap to photograph a letter. Stamp sees only the one image you choose from your gallery. Deadline reminders are made on your phone; nothing is sent to a notification service.

Purchases

The plan is sold through Google Play (or the App Store) and checked through RevenueCat, which receives the random install ID and your purchase receipt. See https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

Not legal advice

Stamp explains what a letter says and drafts a reply for you to check. It is not legal advice and can be wrong. When a lot depends on a letter, ask an advice centre or a lawyer, and always check dates and amounts against the letter itself.

What Stamp does not do

No ads, no analytics, no tracking across apps, no selling of data, no account.

Children

Stamp is not directed at children under 16.

Your rights

Briqvo (Abdullah Ventures LLC) is responsible for the data described in this policy (the "controller" under EU and UK data-protection law). We use it only to provide the app you chose to use, and to keep its free limits fair and stop abuse. Wherever you live, you can ask us what data we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it, by writing to briqvo@gmail.com; because almost everything stays on your phone, we usually hold nothing that identifies you. If you live in the European Economic Area or the UK, you can also complain to your local data-protection authority. Our service providers (Google (Play and Play Billing), RevenueCat, Cloudflare and Anthropic) may process data in the United States and other countries, under their own data-protection terms.
