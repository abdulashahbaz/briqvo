Pop privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Pop is made by Briqvo (Abdullah Ventures LLC). Contact: briqvo@gmail.com.

The short version

A page you make lives inside its own link. Nothing you type is uploaded to us, and there is no account.

How a page works

When you make a page, the names, the question and the look are packed into the link itself, in the part after "#". Browsers do not send that part to any server. The page that opens is a single file that reads the link on the recipient's device and shows it. We never receive the names, the question or the answer.

What Pop keeps

The pages you made are listed on your phone only, so you can copy a link again. Clearing the list in Settings, or uninstalling Pop, removes them. A link you already sent keeps working, because the page is in the link.

The hosted page

The page is served as a plain file. It has no cookies, no trackers and loads nothing from other sites. Like any website, the company that hosts the file may log the address of the device that asks for the file; it cannot see what the page says.

Who sees a link

Anyone who has the link can open the page and read the names and the question in it. Send it only to the person it is for.

Purchases

Pop Plus is sold through Google Play (or the App Store) and checked through RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase receipt. See https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

What Pop does not do

No ads, no analytics, no tracking across apps, no selling of data, no account.

Children

Pop is not directed at children under 13.

Your rights

Briqvo (Abdullah Ventures LLC) is responsible for the data described in this policy (the "controller" under EU and UK data-protection law). We use it only to provide the app you chose to use. Wherever you live, you can ask us what data we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it, by writing to briqvo@gmail.com; because almost everything stays on your phone, we usually hold nothing that identifies you. If you live in the European Economic Area or the UK, you can also complain to your local data-protection authority. Our service providers (Google (Play and Play Billing), RevenueCat) may process data in the United States and other countries, under their own data-protection terms.
