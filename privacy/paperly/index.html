Paperly privacy policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Paperly is made by Briqvo (Abdullah Ventures LLC). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the Paperly app for Android.

The short version

  • Your documents, scans, signatures and the text Paperly recognizes stay on your phone. Paperly has no account and no cloud copy, and never uploads them.
  • Paperly shows ads (from Google AdMob) unless you buy "Remove ads". Ads may use your device's advertising ID and similar data, and where the law requires it we ask for your consent first.
  • Purchases go through Google Play. We use RevenueCat to check whether you own "Remove ads".

What stays on your phone

Everything you scan or import, the PDFs and images Paperly makes, your folders, your saved signatures, and the text recognized in your documents are stored only in Paperly's private storage on your phone. Uninstalling Paperly deletes them. Files you choose to share or save elsewhere (Downloads, your gallery, other apps) are then handled by those places.

Scanning uses Google's ML Kit document scanner, and text recognition uses ML Kit text recognition. Both run on your phone through Google Play services; your documents are not sent to us or to Google for processing. Google Play services may download ML Kit models and may send Google anonymous metrics about how these features perform, under Google's terms.

Paperly does not ask for camera, contacts or location permissions. On Android 8 and 9 it asks for storage access only when you save a file outside the app.

Ads

The free version shows a banner on the library and, at most once per session, a full-screen ad after you finish exporting a document. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, general device information, an approximate location derived from your IP address, and how you interact with ads. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, and wherever else the law requires, Paperly shows Google's consent form before any ad is requested, and you can change your choice any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

How Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Purchases

"Remove ads" is a one-time purchase through Google Play; we never see your payment details. RevenueCat, our purchase service, receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history for Paperly so the app knows that ads are off, including after you reinstall. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your personal information.
  • We don't read, collect or store your documents.
  • Paperly has no analytics of its own at this time. If that changes, this policy will say so before it ships.

Children

Paperly is not directed at children under 13, and ad requests are limited to content suitable for general audiences (rated PG).

Your choices and rights

You can delete any document in the app at any time, change ad consent in Settings, reset your advertising ID, or buy Remove ads to stop ads entirely. For any privacy question or request, including deleting purchase records held by RevenueCat, contact us at briqvo@gmail.com.
