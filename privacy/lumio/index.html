Lumio privacy policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Lumio is made by Briqvo (Abdullah Ventures LLC). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the Lumio app for Android.

The short version

  • To make your AI photos, the photos you pick are sent to our server and from there to Google's Gemini image model. Uploaded photos are deleted within 24 hours: we never keep them. Our server holds them only in memory while your photos are being made.
  • Your results are stored on our server for 7 days so the app can download them, then deleted. The copies on your phone stay until you delete them.
  • There's no sign-up. Lumio doesn't ask for your name, email, phone number or contacts.
  • Credit packs are bought through Google Play. We use RevenueCat to handle purchases.

The photos you upload

When you create photos, Lumio sends the photos you picked (resized to at most 1600 pixels), the style you chose and your preset options (for example "Blazer" and "Studio grey") to our server. You never type a prompt; the server turns your choices into instructions for the model.

  • Our server (on Cloudflare) keeps uploaded photos only in memory for the few seconds it takes to make your results. They are never written to storage, logs or backups, and are gone well within 24 hours.
  • Google, our image model provider, receives your photos and the instructions to generate the result. We use the paid Gemini API with interaction storage turned off. Under Google's terms for the paid service, Google does not use this data to train its models; it may keep requests for a limited time only to detect abuse and meet legal obligations. See Gemini API terms.

Before anything is uploaded, Lumio checks your photos on your phone (is there one face, is it sharp and well lit) using Google ML Kit, which runs entirely on the device.

Please only upload photos of yourself, or of people who gave you permission.

Your results

Generated photos are stored on our server (Cloudflare R2) for 7 days under a random, unguessable address so the app can download them, and are then deleted automatically. The app keeps its own copy, plus the first photo you uploaded (for the before/after comparison), in its private storage on your phone. Deleting a set in the app, or uninstalling Lumio, removes them from your phone. Photos you save go to your gallery (Pictures/Lumio) and stay there.

The free preview is saved on your phone small and with a watermark.

Reports

If you report a result, Lumio sends the result's ID, the reason you chose from a list, your Lumio account ID and the time. We review reports (and the reported result, while it still exists) to remove harmful content and improve our safety filters. Reviewed reports are deleted.

Your Lumio account and credits

Lumio has no sign-up. The first time you use it, our server creates a random account ID and keeps, for that ID: your credit balance, a history of credit changes (purchases, photos created, refunds), whether you've used the free preview, and recent generation times (for rate limits). The ID is stored on your phone, so credits belong to Lumio on this phone and don't carry over to a reinstall or a new phone.

To protect the service from abuse, Lumio uses Google Play Integrity to check that it is the genuine app from Google Play on a genuine Android device. Google Play provides this check; our server only receives the verdict.

Our server's provider (Cloudflare) handles network information such as IP addresses to deliver requests and stop attacks, and our server keeps short-lived error logs that never contain your photos.

Purchases

Credit packs are bought through Google Play; we never see your payment details. RevenueCat, our purchase service, receives your Lumio account ID and your purchase history for Lumio, and tells our server which pack to credit. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your personal information or share it for advertising. Lumio has no ads.
  • We don't keep your uploaded photos, use them for anything except making your results, or use them to train AI models.
  • We don't create styles that turn you into a real public figure, and we don't accept free-text prompts.
  • Lumio has no analytics of its own at this time. If that changes, this policy will say so before it ships.

Children

Lumio is not directed at children under 13.

Your choices and rights

You can delete your results from the app at any time; our server deletes its copies after 7 days anyway. For any privacy question or request, including deleting your account record (balance and history) or purchase records held by RevenueCat, contact us at briqvo@gmail.com. Include the Support ID shown at the bottom of Lumio's Settings so we can find your record.
