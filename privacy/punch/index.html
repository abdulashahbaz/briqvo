Punch privacy policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Punch is made by Briqvo (Abdullah Ventures LLC). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the Punch app for Android and iPhone.

The short version

  • Your videos stay on your phone. Recording, cutting, zooming, captions and exporting all happen on your device.
  • When you tap the one-tap edit, only the sound of that clip is sent to our server and on to Groq, our speech-to-text provider, to get the words and their timing for the captions. It's held in memory while it's read and then dropped.
  • There's no sign-up. Punch doesn't ask for your name, email or phone number. No ads, no analytics.

On your phone only

Your clips, the words heard in them, your edits (cuts, zooms, caption style, word fixes, trims) and the videos you export are kept in the app's private storage on your phone. Exported videos are saved to your gallery (Movies/Punch on Android) when you export. Deleting a clip in Punch removes it and its edit from the app; videos already exported stay in your gallery. Uninstalling Punch deletes everything Punch keeps. We have no copy, so we can't recover it for you.

Camera and microphone

Punch asks for the camera and microphone only when you open the record screen, after a one-line explanation, and uses them only while that screen is open. Importing uses your phone's own photo picker: Punch sees only the video you pick and needs no access to your gallery. Saving an export adds the file to your gallery; on Android 10 and later this needs no permission.

The one-tap edit

When you tap the one-tap edit, Punch takes the clip's audio track and sends it, with the clip's length, to our server:

  • Our server (on Cloudflare) holds the audio only in memory while it's transcribed (usually a few seconds). It is never written to storage, logs or backups. It counts the edit against your plan's limit, tied to your random account ID.
  • Groq, our speech-to-text provider, receives the audio and returns the words with their timing. See Groq's privacy policy.

The words come back to your phone and are kept there with your clip. The video itself is never uploaded.

Record and edit only videos you have the right to use, and don't upload other people's voices without their permission.

Your Punch account

Punch has no sign-up. Our server creates a random account ID the first time the app connects, and keeps for it: when it was made, when you made one-tap edits in the last week (for the free plan and fair-use limits) and whether your plan is active. The ID is stored on your phone.

Cloudflare handles network information such as IP addresses to deliver requests and stop attacks, and our server keeps short-lived error logs that never contain your audio or words.

Plans and purchases

Plans are bought through Google Play or the App Store; we never see your payment details. RevenueCat, our subscription service, receives your account ID and your purchase history for Punch, and our server asks RevenueCat whether your plan is active. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

If you start a free trial, Punch sets a reminder on your phone for 2 days before you're charged. It's a local notification: no push service is involved.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your information or share it for advertising. Punch has no ads and no analytics.
  • We don't upload or keep your videos, and we don't keep your audio.
  • We don't use your audio or words to train AI.

Children

Punch is not directed at children under 13 and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices and rights

You can delete any clip in the app at any time, and uninstalling removes everything on your phone. To ask about the little we keep on the server (your account ID, edit counts and plan status), or to have it deleted, email us with the account ID shown in a support request; depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California) you have rights to access, correct and delete your information, and to complain to your data protection authority.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
