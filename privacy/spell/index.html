Spell privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Spell is made by Briqvo (Abdullah Ventures LLC). Contact: briqvo@gmail.com.

The short version

Spell holds text prompts. It never sees your photos: you paste a prompt into your own AI app and add your photo there. The free version shows ads, and the ad service collects some data from your device to do that.

What Spell keeps

The prompts you saved, on your phone only. Clear them in Settings; uninstalling Spell removes them. There is no account.

Your photos

Spell does not ask for your photos, camera or gallery. When you tap "Open in" an AI app, Spell only opens that app or website. What you do there is covered by that service's own terms and privacy policy.

Ads

The free version shows a banner from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google collects data such as your device's advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, and how you interact with ads. See https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Spell asks for your choice with Google's consent form before any ad is requested, and you can change it any time in Settings, Ad choices.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.
  • Buying "Remove ads" stops ads being requested.

Purchases

"Remove ads" is sold through Google Play (or the App Store) and checked through RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase receipt. See https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

What Spell does not do

No account, no upload of your photos, no analytics SDK of our own, no selling of data by us.

Not affiliated

Spell is an independent app. It is not affiliated with Google, OpenAI or any AI service; their names are used only to say where a prompt can be pasted.

Children

Spell is not directed at children under 13.

Your rights

Briqvo (Abdullah Ventures LLC) is responsible for the data described in this policy (the "controller" under EU and UK data-protection law). We use it only to provide the app you chose to use; ads in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are shown only on the basis of the choice you make in the consent form. Wherever you live, you can ask us what data we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it, by writing to briqvo@gmail.com; because almost everything stays on your phone, we usually hold nothing that identifies you. If you live in the European Economic Area or the UK, you can also complain to your local data-protection authority. Our service providers (Google (Play, Play Billing and AdMob), RevenueCat) may process data in the United States and other countries, under their own data-protection terms.
