Spell privacy policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Spell is made by Briqvo (Abdullah Ventures LLC). Contact: briqvo@gmail.com.

The short version

Spell holds text prompts. It never sees your photos: you paste a prompt into your own AI app and add your photo there. Spell has no ads, no account and no analytics, and it sends nothing about you anywhere.

What Spell keeps

The prompts you saved, on your phone only. Clear them in Settings; uninstalling Spell removes them. There is no account.

Your photos

Spell does not ask for your photos, camera or gallery. When you tap "Open in" an AI app, Spell only opens that app or website. What you do there is covered by that service's own terms and privacy policy.

What Spell does not do

No ads, no account, no upload of your photos, no analytics, no tracking, no selling of data.

Not affiliated

Spell is an independent app. It is not affiliated with Google, OpenAI or any AI service; their names are used only to say where a prompt can be pasted.

Children

Spell is not directed at children under 13.

Your rights

Briqvo (Abdullah Ventures LLC) is responsible for this app (the "controller" under EU and UK data-protection law). Spell keeps your saved prompts on your phone and sends us nothing, so we hold no data that identifies you. Wherever you live, you can still ask us what data we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it, by writing to briqvo@gmail.com. If you live in the European Economic Area or the UK, you can also complain to your local data-protection authority. Google Play, which delivers the app, processes data under its own terms.

Changes

If Spell ever adds ads or purchases, this policy will say so first, with a new date, and the app will ask for any choice the law requires before it changes anything.
