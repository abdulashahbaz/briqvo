Basil privacy policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Basil is made by Briqvo (Abdullah Ventures LLC). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the Basil app for Android and iPhone.

The short version

  • Your plants, their photos, check-ups and watering reminders stay on your phone. We never receive them.
  • When you diagnose a plant, that photo is sent to our server and from there to our AI provider to work out what's wrong. For a check-up, the plant's first photo and today's photo are sent together, with the first diagnosis's short headline. Our server never stores them.
  • There's no sign-up. Basil doesn't ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Plans are bought through Google Play or the App Store. We use RevenueCat to handle subscriptions.

On your phone only

Your plants (names, care plans, watering dates), their photos, diagnoses and check-ups, and the fix steps you've ticked off are kept in the app's private storage on your phone. Deleting a plant deletes its photos. Uninstalling Basil deletes all of it. We have no copy, so we can't recover it for you.

Reminders (watering, light and check-up days, and a trial reminder if you start a trial) are local notifications scheduled on your phone. No push service is involved.

Diagnoses and check-ups

When you take or pick a photo to diagnose, Basil resizes it (at most 1024 pixels) and sends it to our server. For a check-up, it also sends the plant's first photo and the first diagnosis's plant name, headline and health score, so the two can be compared. Nothing else from your plants goes with them.

  • Our server (on Cloudflare) holds the photos only in memory while they are read, a few seconds. They are never written to storage, logs or backups.
  • Cloudflare Workers AI runs the AI for everyone, including free trials, unless a provider below is used for a paying subscriber. These are open AI models hosted by Cloudflare on its own network. It receives the photos and our instructions and sends back the result. Cloudflare states that it doesn't use this content to train AI models. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.
  • For paying subscribers, we may instead use: Anthropic (Claude), which receives the photos and our instructions, and sends back the diagnosis. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, data sent through its API is not used to train its models; Anthropic may keep requests for a limited time for safety and legal reasons. See Anthropic's privacy policy.

Please photograph plants, not people.

Your Basil account

Basil has no sign-up. The first time you diagnose a plant, our server creates a random account ID and keeps, for that ID: whether you've used the free diagnosis, how many checks you've made today and in the last hour (for the daily limit), and whether your plan is active. The ID is stored on your phone.

Our server's provider (Cloudflare) handles network information such as IP addresses to deliver requests and stop attacks, and our server keeps short-lived error logs that never contain your photos.

Plans and purchases

Plans are bought through Google Play or the App Store; we never see your payment details. RevenueCat, our subscription service, receives your Basil account ID and your purchase history for Basil, and our server asks RevenueCat whether your plan is active. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

If you start a free trial, Basil sets a reminder on your phone for 2 days before you're charged.

Sharing cards

If you share a health score card or a before/after card, Basil makes the image on your phone and hands it to the app you choose. We don't receive it.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your information or share it for advertising. Basil has no ads.
  • We don't receive your plants, their photos (except to read them for a diagnosis or check-up you ask for), or your reminders.
  • Basil has no analytics of its own at this time. If that changes, this policy will say so before it ships.

Advice, not a guarantee

Basil gives general plant-care guidance from photos, and it can be wrong. It always shows how sure it is and suggests asking a local nursery when it isn't sure. For valuable plants, food crops, or anything that may be toxic to pets or people, ask an expert.

Your choices and rights

You can delete any plant in the app at any time, or everything by uninstalling. To delete the account record our server keeps (free diagnosis used, today's count) or purchase records held by RevenueCat, contact us at briqvo@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date before the change takes effect.
