Undo privacy policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Undo is made by Briqvo (Abdullah Ventures LLC). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the Undo app for Android and iPhone.

The short version

  • Everything you put into Undo stays on your phone. Your puff log, what you told us about your vaping and what it costs, your reasons for quitting, your plan, your milestones and your quit buddy's name and code are kept in the app's private storage. We never receive them.
  • Undo has no account, no sign-up and no server of its own. It doesn't ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Undo Pro is bought through Google Play or the App Store. We use RevenueCat to handle purchases.
  • No ads. No analytics. No tracking.

On your phone only

Puffs you log (in the app or with the home-screen widget), cravings you ride out in SOS, your answers from onboarding (puffs on a usual day, weekly cost, your reasons and anything you wrote in your own words), your step-down plan and settings are stored on your phone. The home-screen widget keeps today's count and any puffs tapped on it in the app's private storage until the app adds them to your log.

Uninstalling Undo deletes all of it, and so does Settings → Start over. We have no copy, so we can't recover it for you.

Sharing, and quit buddies

Nothing leaves your phone unless you choose to share it:

  • Share cards are images made on your phone (your streak or the money you haven't spent). They go wherever you send them with your phone's share sheet.
  • Quit buddy invites and check-ins are messages you send with your phone's share sheet. An invite contains your six-letter buddy code and a link to Undo on Google Play. A check-in contains the numbers shown in the message before you send it. Undo doesn't send anything by itself and has no way to see your buddy's progress.
  • If you install Undo from a buddy's invite link on Android, Google Play passes the buddy code in the link to Undo when it first opens (the "install referrer"), so Undo can fill it in for you. It stays on your phone.

Purchases

When you open the Undo Pro screen or buy Pro, RevenueCat receives a random anonymous ID made by its SDK, your purchase history from Google Play or the App Store, and basic device information (such as the app version and your country's store) to show prices and check whether Pro is active. RevenueCat does not receive your puff log or anything you typed in Undo. See RevenueCat's privacy policy. Google Play and the App Store handle payment; we never see your card details.

Notifications

If you start a free trial, Undo asks to send notifications and schedules one reminder on your phone, two days before the trial turns into a paid plan. It is a local notification: no push service is used.

Children

Undo is for adults (18+). It asks your age when you start and doesn't continue for anyone under 18. We don't knowingly collect information from children, and Undo collects nothing about anyone in any case.

Health

Undo helps you track and cut down. It is not a medical device and doesn't give medical advice or treatment. For help quitting, talk to a doctor or pharmacist, or your national health service's quit support (Settings → Quit help).

Your rights

Because your information never leaves your phone, you control it fully: edit it in Settings, delete it with Start over, or uninstall the app. For purchase records, contact us and we'll help you ask RevenueCat to delete them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and the policy in the app's store listing.
