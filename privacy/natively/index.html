Natively privacy policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Natively is made by Briqvo (Abdullah Ventures LLC). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the Natively app for Android and iPhone.

The short version

  • When you talk with your tutor, each answer you record is sent to our server, turned into text, and answered. Your recordings are never stored: they exist only in memory while that one answer is processed.
  • Your level, first language, practice history, streaks, saved phrases and speaking check-ins stay on your phone.
  • Speaking check-ins are never sent anywhere. They are recorded and kept on your phone, and you choose whether to share them.
  • There's no sign-up. Natively doesn't ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Plans are bought through Google Play or the App Store. We use RevenueCat to handle subscriptions.

Conversations with your tutor

When you tap the microphone and answer, Natively records that answer (up to 60 seconds) and sends it to our server, with the roleplay you're practising, your level, your first language, your settings for explanations and voice, and the text of the conversation so far. If you type instead, the typed text is sent.

  • Our server (on Cloudflare) holds the recording only in memory while it is processed, a few seconds. It is never written to storage, logs or backups. It counts how many seconds you spoke, for the daily limit.
  • Cloudflare Workers AI runs the AI for everyone, including free trials, unless a provider below is used for a paying subscriber. These are open AI models hosted by Cloudflare on its own network. It receives your recording (to turn it into words), the conversation's text (for the tutor's reply) and the reply's text (for the voice you hear) and sends back the result. Cloudflare states that it doesn't use this content to train AI models. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.
  • For paying subscribers, we may instead use: Deepgram (speech-to-text), which receives the recording and returns the words you said. See Deepgram's privacy policy.
  • For paying subscribers, we may instead use: Anthropic (Claude), which receives the text of the conversation (never the recording) and writes your tutor's reply and any correction. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, data sent through its API is not used to train its models. See Anthropic's privacy policy.
  • For paying subscribers, we may instead use: Google Cloud Text-to-Speech, which receives the text of your tutor's reply (not anything you said) and returns the audio you hear. See Google's privacy policy.

These providers may keep requests for a limited time for safety, abuse prevention and legal reasons, under their own terms. Please don't share personal details such as your full name, address or ID numbers in a conversation: the roleplays never need them, and made-up details work just as well.

On your phone only

Your level, first language, goal and settings; the list of conversations you've had (date, roleplay, minutes, number of corrections), your streak, the phrases saved from corrections, and your speaking check-in recordings are kept in the app's private storage on your phone. Deleting a check-in deletes its recording. Uninstalling Natively deletes all of it. We have no copy, so we can't recover it for you.

Microphone

Natively asks for the microphone the first time you start a conversation or a check-in, after explaining why. It listens only while you're recording an answer (the button shows it), never in the background. You can turn the microphone off at any time in your phone's settings and type your answers instead.

Your Natively account

Natively has no sign-up. The first time you use it, our server creates a random account ID and keeps, for that ID: how many seconds you've spoken today, which conversation is today's free roleplay, and whether your plan is active. The ID is stored on your phone.

Our server's provider (Cloudflare) handles network information such as IP addresses to deliver requests and stop attacks, and our server keeps short-lived error logs that never contain your recordings or conversations.

Plans and purchases

Plans are bought through Google Play or the App Store; we never see your payment details. RevenueCat, our subscription service, receives your Natively account ID and your purchase history for Natively, and our server asks RevenueCat whether your plan is active. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

If you start a free trial, Natively sets a reminder on your phone for 2 days before you're charged. It's a local notification: no push service is involved.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your information or share it for advertising. Natively has no ads.
  • We don't store your recordings, or use them for anything except understanding that answer.
  • We don't receive your check-ins, streaks or saved phrases.
  • Natively has no analytics of its own at this time. If that changes, this policy will say so before it ships.

Age

Natively is for people aged 13 and over.

Your choices and rights

You can delete check-ins and saved phrases in the app at any time, or everything by uninstalling. To delete the account record our server keeps (seconds spoken today, today's free roleplay) or purchase records held by RevenueCat, contact us at briqvo@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date before the change takes effect.
