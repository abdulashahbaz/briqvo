Delete your data

Briqvo apps keep your documents, photos, tasks and settings on your phone. Uninstalling an app deletes everything it stored there.

Ask us to delete data held off your phone

Some apps use services that keep a small amount of data: purchase records (RevenueCat), shared groups (Settly) and photos being processed (Lumio). To have it deleted, email briqvo@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data" and tell us:

  • the app's name (Paperly, Sweepo, Stikko, DoFirst, Panora, Settly, Lumio, PassPic or Tinted)
  • the email address of the Google account you bought with, if you made a purchase
  • for Settly, the names of your groups

We delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm. Ad data held by Google can be reset or deleted in your phone's Google settings (Ads).

Salat Time doesn't collect or share any data, so there's nothing to delete.
